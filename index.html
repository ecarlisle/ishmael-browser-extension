Help & Privacy

Understanding how Ishmael uses your data and how to configure your reading experience.

Settings Help

Voice ID
Selects the specific acoustic model used for synthesis. Different IDs provide distinct tonal qualities.
Models
Determines the underlying AI generation engine. Newer models generally offer more natural phrasing but may have higher latency.
Moods
Applies prosodic adjustments to the voice output. None keeps the natural tone.

Privacy & Data

Your data stays with you.

  • Local storage: your API key and preferences are stored only on this device.
  • No analytics: Ishmael does not track your reading habits or use trackers.
  • Fish Audio integration: page text is sent directly to Fish Audio's API to generate speech. No text is stored by Ishmael.

Review Fish Audio's privacy policy for details on how they process requests.

Troubleshooting

Narration won't start

The background audio context was suspended by the browser. Reopen the popup and try again.

API quota exceeded

Your Fish Audio API key has run out of credits. Check your account dashboard.

Network timeout

Failed to reach the synthesis server. Check your connection or try a different voice model.
